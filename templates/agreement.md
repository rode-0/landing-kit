# Website agreement

*A simple, plain-language template. It isn't legal advice; laws differ by state, so have someone qualified look it over if you're unsure.*

---

**Between** [Your name or business] ("I") **and** [Client business] ("you").
**Date:** [date]

**1. What I'll make.** A one-page website for [Client business] with your logo, photos, services, hours and contact details, working on phones and computers. Includes [2] rounds of changes before launch. Each extra round of changes is $[amount], and I'll tell you before starting one.

**2. Price and payment.**
- New website: $[amount]. Half ($[amount]) is due before I start. The rest is due when the site goes live.
- Monthly care: $[amount] a month, starting the month the site goes live. Covers small updates (hours, prices, notices, photos, text) and keeping the site online. Larger changes are quoted first.
- Invoices are due within [14] days.

**3. What you provide.** Your answers to my questions, your logo, and photos you own or have permission to use. You confirm the facts on the site are correct before launch.

**4. Your domain.** You buy and own your domain name, in your name. I'll help you connect it.

**5. Ownership.** Once the website is paid for in full, the site's content is yours: your words, photos and logo, and the finished page. If you stop the care plan, I'll give you a copy of the site's files.

**6. Ending the care plan.** Either of us can stop the monthly care plan with [30] days' notice.

**7. Timing.** I'll aim to deliver the first draft within [5] business days of receiving your answers and photos.

Signed:

______________________  [Your name], [date]

______________________  [Client name], [Client business], [date]
