# Messages you can send

Change the parts in [brackets]. Keep it short. Always mention something specific about their business.

## Email

**Subject:** A website for [Business]

Hi [Name],

I'm [your name], and I make websites for local businesses in [town]. I'm a regular at [Business] / I came across [Business] and noticed [you don't have a website yet / your site doesn't show your hours on a phone].

Here's one I made recently: [link to a sample]. It shows what to expect: works on phones, shows your hours and prices, and has a button to [call / book / order].

Would you like me to put together a quick draft for [Business]? No obligation.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone]

## Text (for someone you know)

Hi [Name], it's [your name]. I've started making websites for local businesses. Would you be up for one for [Business]? Here's an example: [link]. Happy to show you a draft first.

## Instagram or Facebook message

Hi! I love what you're doing at [Business]. I make simple, modern websites for local businesses, and I noticed you don't have one yet. Here's an example: [link]. Want me to make a quick draft for you to look at?

## Follow-up (one week later, once)

Hi [Name], just following up on my note about a website for [Business]. If now's not a good time, no problem at all. If you'd like to see a draft, just reply and I'll send one over.

## After launch: asking for a referral

Thanks again, [Name]. I'm really glad the site is live. If you know another business owner who could use a website, I'd be grateful for an introduction. And if you have a minute, a short review on Google means a lot to a small business like mine: [link].
