# Messages you can send

Change the parts in [brackets]. Keep it short. Always mention something specific about their business.

Don't offer free drafts. Your sample sites and your own site are the demo; work on their site starts after the deposit.

## Email

**Subject:** A website for [Business]

Hi [Name],

I'm [your name], and I make websites for local businesses in [town]. I'm a regular at [Business] / I came across [Business] and noticed [you don't have a website yet / your site doesn't show your hours on a phone].

Here are a few I've made: [your site's link]. It shows what to expect: works on phones, shows your hours and prices, and has a button to [call / book / order].

Would you be open to a 15-minute chat? I can show you what a site for [Business] would include and what it costs.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone]

## Text (for someone you know)

Hi [Name], it's [your name]. I've started making websites for local businesses. Would you be up for one for [Business]? Here are some examples: [your site's link]. Happy to show you more in person.

## Instagram or Facebook message

Hi! I love what you're doing at [Business]. I make simple, modern websites for local businesses, and I noticed you don't have one yet. Here are some examples: [your site's link]. Open to a quick chat about one for [Business]?

## Follow-up (one week later, once)

Hi [Name], just following up on my note about a website for [Business]. If now's not a good time, no problem at all. If you'd like to talk it over, just reply and we'll find a time.

## After launch: asking for a referral

Thanks again, [Name]. I'm really glad the site is live. If you know another business owner who could use a website, I'd be grateful for an introduction. And if you have a minute, a short review on Google means a lot to a small business like mine: [link].
