# Design directions

Pick the one that fits the customer brief. Each has starting fonts (Google Fonts) and a color approach.
Change them to fit the business; these are starting points, not a uniform.

## Warm editorial
Soft serif display, paper-toned background, generous captions. Bakeries, makers, inns, independent shops.
Fonts: Libre Caslon Display + Schibsted Grotesk; Young Serif + Figtree; Spectral + Hanken Grotesk.
Color: off-white or flour-gray paper, near-black ink, one accent taken from the product.
Graphics: fine pen-line drawings of the product (a loaf, a cup, a sprig), a paper texture, stamp-like marks.
Watch out: cream background + big serif is the most common AI look. Earn it.

## Clean Swiss
One strong grotesk, strict grid, white or near-black, one clear accent, numbers set large.
Clinics, trades, accountants, tech, anything where clarity is the point.
Fonts: Schibsted Grotesk + Onest; Familjen Grotesk + Figtree; Archivo + Archivo (two weights).
Color: white or near-black, one accent from the brand or the photos.
Graphics: geometric line work (a single bold rule, a grid of dots, one simple diagram of how the service works), solid color blocks.

## Gallery
Almost no text, photos large and edge to edge, small labels, lots of empty space.
Architects, photographers, florists, artists.
Fonts: Archivo (wide) + Literata; Spectral Light + Schibsted Grotesk.
Color: the photos are the color. Cool white or warm gray, one quiet accent.
Graphics: almost none. A thin line monogram and one quiet linear motif; let the logo and photos breathe.

## Bold local
Oversized condensed type, two strong flat colors, chunky rules, playful but tidy.
Groomers, cafes with attitude, gyms, food trucks, kids' services.
Fonts: Big Shoulders Display + Figtree; Anton + Onest; Archivo Narrow + Red Hat Text.
Color: two confident flat colors taken from the brand or photos, plus paper and ink.
Graphics: thick single-weight line drawings of the trade's tools, a stamp or badge-style logo, flat color shapes behind photos.

## Classic trust
Refined serif, small caps, deep color fields, formal symmetry.
Law, finance, family businesses with history, funeral homes.
Fonts: Playfair Display SC + Source Serif 4; Bodoni Moda + Hanken Grotesk; Gloock + Literata.
Color: deep navy, oxblood or forest, with cream and a metallic-feeling neutral.
Graphics: a monogram or crest-like mark, fine rules and corners, an engraved-feel line motif.

## Soft modern
Rounded or humanist sans, gentle tints, one generous radius, calm motion.
Wellness, therapy, kids, community groups.
Fonts: Figtree + Figtree; Red Hat Display + Red Hat Text; Onest + Literata.
Color: soft tints with one deeper anchor color for text and buttons.
Graphics: soft organic line drawings (a wave, a leaf, a sun), rounded color shapes behind photos.

## Fonts to avoid
Inter, Poppins, Montserrat, Roboto, Open Sans, Space Grotesk (too common). Fraunces, Instrument Serif and
Newsreader are overused right now; use them only with a reason.
