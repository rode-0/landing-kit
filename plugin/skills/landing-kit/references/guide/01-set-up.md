# 1. Set up

Start small. **Level 1** is everything you need to finish one paid job. Do Level 2 and Level 3 as you go.

**Easiest way to follow along:** open the setup page, https://rode-0.github.io/landing-kit/tools/setup/. It's this same list with tick boxes, and it shows what to do next. Your ticks are saved in that browser.

## Level 1 · Finish your first paid job (about 30 minutes)

1. **Claude account.** Sign up at claude.ai. A paid plan is needed for regular use.
2. **Turn on code execution.** In Claude's settings, under Capabilities, turn on code execution. The kit needs it for photos, checks and previews.
3. **Add the Landing Kit.** In Claude, open **Customize**, then **Plugins**, then **Add**, then **Add marketplace**, and type `rode-0/landing-kit`. Add **Landing Kit** from the list. It updates itself when the kit improves. (On a work or company Claude account, ask the person who manages it to add it for everyone.)
4. **Model.** In each chat, pick **Claude Opus 5.5** or a newer Opus. The kit was built and tested with Opus.
5. **Netlify.** Create a free account at netlify.com. This is where client sites go online.
6. **Your prices.** Open the pricing page (https://rode-0.github.io/landing-kit/tools/pricing/). Fill in your own hours and costs; it suggests prices and writes a quote.
7. **Your agreement.** In Claude, say "Use the landing kit to fill in my agreement" and give your name, prices, included rounds and how many small updates the care plan covers.
8. **Getting paid for one job.** The invoice maker (https://rode-0.github.io/landing-kit/tools/invoice/) is enough: the client pays by bank transfer, check or a payment link.
9. **Business basics.** Check your city and state rules for a business license or registered name, keep a separate bank account if you can, and ask an accountant about taxes. (This kit isn't legal or tax advice.)

To start a client, open a new chat and say **"Use the landing kit to build a website for [business]"**.

## Level 2 · Get organised (about 30 minutes)

10. **Your tracker.** In Claude, ask "Use the landing kit to give me the tracker", or download it from https://rode-0.github.io/landing-kit/tools/tracker/tracker.xlsx. Upload it to Google Sheets (File, then Import) or open it in Excel. Every client, payment and round lives here, and each client gets a "Next step".
11. **Your links.** Your question page for clients is https://rode-0.github.io/landing-kit/tools/intake/?for=YourName (put your own name at the end). Save it and your portfolio link on the setup page.
12. **Card payments.** Open a Square, Stripe or PayPal account so clients can pay by card, including automatic monthly billing for care plans.
13. **Your hosting budget.** All your clients' sites live in your Netlify account. If its monthly credits run out, **every site you host pauses** (visitors see "Site not available") until the next month, unless you upgrade or turn on auto recharge. Move to the $9 Personal plan once you have a few clients, check usage once a month, and decide whether to turn on auto recharge and with what monthly limit.
14. **Gmail (optional).** Connect Gmail to Claude in claude.ai's settings, under connectors. Claude can then read a client's emails for details and draft your replies. It never sends anything for you.

## Level 3 · Win more clients (about 2 to 4 hours)

15. **Two sample sites.** Build sites for two different kinds of business (a friend's or family member's), labelled "Sample".
16. **Your own site.** In a new chat, say "Use the landing kit to build my own site" and attach screenshots of your samples. You get a portfolio page with your examples, your packages, your phone and email, and a short enquiry form.
17. **Test it on your phone.** Send yourself a message from the enquiry form, tap your phone number and email, and open every example.
18. **Social posts (optional).** Say "Use the landing kit to make my marketing plan", then schedule the posts with the platform's own scheduler (for Facebook and Instagram, Meta Business Suite).

**Not sure what to do next?** In Claude, ask "Use the landing kit: what do I do next?" and paste your tracker's Dashboard. The setup page has a button that writes the question for you.
