# Who owns what

| Service | Who sets it up | Who pays | Who looks after it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude plan and the Landing Kit plugin | You | You | You |
| Netlify account (hosts every client site) | You | You (covered by care prices) | You: check usage monthly; if credits run out, every site pauses |
| Your tool pages (question page, pricing, preview, invoice, setup) | Nobody: they're online at a fixed address | Free | The kit's maintainer |
| Tracker (Google Sheets or Excel) | You | Free | You: log every payment, round, update, enquiry and marketing session |
| Card payments (Square, Stripe or PayPal) | You | Fees per payment | You |
| Client's domain | The client buys it, in their name; you help connect it | The client | The client renews it; your tracker warns you 60 days ahead |
| Client's email on their domain | Already the client's | The client | The client. You never change its records |
| Client's booking, store or payment pages | Already the client's (reuse them) | The client | The client. You tap the links at launch and at each check-in |
| Contact form (Web3Forms key) | You, with the client's email | Free up to 250 messages a month | You: send a test message at launch |
| Your own site and social accounts | You | You | You. Organic posts only unless you decide otherwise |
| Automatic posting script (optional) | A technical owner | Their hosting | That technical owner (see extras/scheduler) |
| The kit itself | Its maintainer | Free | The maintainer publishes new versions on the Releases page |

Never share passwords. Ask clients for an invite to their accounts instead.
