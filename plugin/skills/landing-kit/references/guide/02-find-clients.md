# 2. Find clients

**Who:** local, owner-run businesses where the owner decides on the spot. Trades, food, beauty, fitness, shops and services.

**Look for:**
- No website, only Facebook or Instagram.
- A website that looks old or breaks on a phone. Open it on your phone and see.
- Missing basics: no hours, no prices, no way to book.
- Businesses you already know and use.

**First, make two sample sites** (a friend's or family member's business). Showing one on your phone wins more clients than anything you say.

**What to say, in person:** "Hi, I'm [name]. I make websites for local businesses. I noticed [your site doesn't show your hours on a phone]. Here's one I made for [business]. Would something like this help? I can show you a draft of yours before you decide."

Ready-made email, text and Instagram messages are in [`templates/messages.md`](../templates/messages.md).

**Common answers:**
- *"My nephew can do it."* "Great. If that falls through, here's my number."
- *"I have Facebook."* "Facebook is good for regulars. A website is what new customers find on Google, with your hours, prices and how to book in one place."
- *"It's too expensive."* "The care plan includes updates, so you never have to touch it. What's one extra customer a month worth to you?"
- *"Can I change it myself?"* "You don't need to. Text me what changed and I'll update it, usually the same week."

**Track it:** add each business you contact to the tracker with Stage "Lead".

**Your first month:**

| Week | Do this |
|---|---|
| 1 | Set up, make two sample sites, set prices, open a payment account. |
| 2 | List 20 local businesses. Contact the 10 you know best. |
| 3 | Follow up once with anyone who didn't answer. Hold first meetings. |
| 4 | Build and launch your first sites. Ask each client for a referral. Contact the next 10. |
