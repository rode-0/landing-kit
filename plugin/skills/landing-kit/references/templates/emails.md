# Emails to clients, stage by stage

Claude can draft these for you from your Gmail. You always read and send them yourself. Change the parts in [brackets].

## 1. After the first meeting
Subject: Your new website: next steps
Hi [Name], thanks for meeting today. Two things to get started:
1. Answer a few questions here (about 10 minutes): [question page link]
2. Reply with your logo and 4 to 10 photos of your work, your place, and you at work.
I've attached the agreement and the deposit invoice. Once those are in, I'll send your first draft within [5] business days.

## 2. Missing details
Subject: A few quick questions for your website
Hi [Name], I'm putting your site together. Could you tell me: [question 1]? [question 2]? Thanks!

## 3. First draft ready
Subject: Your website draft is ready
Hi [Name], here's a preview of your new site (attached; tap it to open it in your browser). It's marked PREVIEW and the buttons are switched off until it's live. Have a look on your phone and computer, and reply with anything you'd like changed. Your price includes [2] rounds of changes.

## 4. After a round of changes
Subject: Updated website: round [N]
Hi [Name], here's the updated preview (attached). What changed: [short list]. Still waiting on: [anything missing].

## 5. Extra round notice
Subject: About the next round of changes
Hi [Name], happy to make these changes. Your agreement included [2] rounds, so this one is an extra round at $[amount]. Reply "go ahead" and I'll start.

## 6. Approved: final invoice
Subject: Your final invoice, then we go live
Hi [Name], thanks for approving the site. I've attached the final invoice ([amount], including [any extra rounds]). As soon as it's paid I'll put the site online. To use your own web address, buy the domain in your name at [registrar] and send me the login invite, and I'll connect it.

## 7. Live
Subject: Your website is live
Hi [Name], your site is live at [address]. Thanks for your payment. Your monthly care plan starts this month: just email me whenever something changes (hours, prices, photos, a holiday) and I'll update it. If you know another business owner who needs a site, I'd be grateful for an introduction.

## 8. Domain renewal reminder
Subject: Your web address renews soon
Hi [Name], a reminder that [domain] renews on [date]. Check that the card on your registrar account is up to date, so the site doesn't go down.
