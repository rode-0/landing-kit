Do I need a referral?

No. Minnesota allows direct access, so you can book without seeing a doctor first.

Do you take my insurance?

We’re in network with Medica, HealthPartners, Blue Cross Blue Shield of Minnesota and Aetna.

How soon can I get in?

First visits are usually available within 3 days. See open times on the booking page.

Will I see the same therapist?

Yes. Every session is a full 60 minutes with the same therapist, never handed to an aide.

What we treat

  • Sports injury rehab
  • Post-surgery rehab: knee, hip and shoulder
  • Back and neck pain
  • Running assessment with video gait analysis

Who you’ll see

Sam Okafor, DPT

Owner. Board-certified sports clinical specialist.

Every session is a full 60 minutes, one-on-one, with the same therapist from your first visit on.

We work with active adults and weekend athletes in Northeast Minneapolis, and with people recovering from surgery.

Prefer a call back

Tell us what hurts and when to reach you, and the front desk will call you back. To pick a time yourself, book an evaluation online.
