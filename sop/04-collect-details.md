# 4. Collect the details

You need: the client's answers, their logo, and 4 to 10 photos.

- **The question page** (`tools/intake`): the client fills it in on their phone, taps **Copy my answers**, and emails or texts them to you. Add `?for=YourName` to the end of the link to show your name on it.
- **Or by email:** with Gmail connected, the client can just reply to your email. Claude reads the thread for the details.
- **Logo:** ask for the biggest file they have, ideally an SVG or a large PNG from their designer.
- **Photos:** their work up close, their place, them at work, a detail. Phone photos are fine. No stock photos.
- **Save everything** in the client's backup folder (Google Drive or Dropbox) and add the folder link to the tracker.
