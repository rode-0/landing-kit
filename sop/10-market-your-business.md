# 10. Market your business

Use this once you have two sample sites and your own site (setup Level 3). Organic posts only: the kit doesn't include paid ads, and nothing here posts for you.

## 1. Get a plan from Claude (about 30 minutes)

In your Websites Project, start a new chat and say **"Make my marketing plan"**. Tell Claude:
- the one kind of business you want to start with (for example, dog groomers in your town),
- your site's link and which social platform you'll use,
- how many posts a week you can keep up (3 is plenty).

You get back:
- **One line on who you help** and how, in plain words.
- **Three post themes**: before and after (an old site next to the new one, with the owner's OK), how it works (your steps and prices), and a local business spotlight (with permission).
- **A post design** that matches your site: the same colors, fonts and logo, so every post looks like yours.
- **A month of posts**: each with its picture (as a page you save as an image), its caption, the day and time, and a link back to your site's enquiry form.

Check every post before you use it. Claude never makes up reviews, numbers or results.

## 2. Schedule the posts (about 20 minutes a month)

Use the platform's own free scheduler. For Facebook and Instagram that's **Meta Business Suite**: create the post, add the picture and caption, choose **Schedule**, and pick the day and time from Claude's plan. Do the whole month in one sitting.

An automatic posting script (a "cron job") is possible but needs someone technical to set up and look after it, plus approved access to your accounts. See `extras/scheduler/README.md`. You don't need it to get your first clients.

## 3. Track what works

- Log each marketing session on the tracker's Log tab: type **Marketing**, what you did, and the hours.
- Log each enquiry (type **Enquiry**) and meeting (type **Meeting**) with its **Source** (Instagram, referral, walk-in...).
- The Dashboard shows enquiries, meetings and marketing hours this month. After a month, keep what brings enquiries and drop what doesn't.
