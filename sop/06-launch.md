# 6. Launch

1. When the client approves, say **"approved"** in the chat. Claude runs a final check, gives you the final page, the hosting steps, and a row for your tracker's Clients tab.
2. **Download** the page from the artifact menu, name it `index.html`, and put it alone in a folder.
3. **Put it online:** while logged in to Netlify, drag the folder onto app.netlify.com/drop. The site appears in your account; rename it in its settings.
4. **Domain:** help the client buy their domain in their own name (any registrar), then connect it in Netlify under Domain management.
5. **Contact form** (if the site has one): send yourself a test message from the live site.
6. **Email the client** that it's live, with the final invoice (email 7).
7. **Track it:** paste Claude's Clients row, set Stage to "Live" or "Care plan", add the website address, domain and renewal date. Save the final `index.html` in the client's backup folder, named by date.
