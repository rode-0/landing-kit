# 5. Build and review

1. **Start a new chat** in your Websites Project (Opus 5.5 or newer). Paste the client's answers and attach their logo and photos. Add a line with your extra round price (for example "Price per extra round: $60"). If Gmail is connected, add: "Check my emails with [client email] too."
2. **Version 1.** Claude writes a short brief about the client's customers, builds the page, checks it, and shows it to you. Want options first? Say "show me concepts" for three quick layout ideas.
3. **Show the client.** Download the page from Claude, name it `vitos-preview.html` (the client's name plus "preview"), and send it to the owner as an email or text attachment (email 3 in `templates/emails.md`). They tap it and it opens in their browser, on a phone or a computer. Or show it on your own phone in person. Claude won't make a public link for a page with a real business's name and details, so don't ask it to; the file is private to you and the owner. The contact form won't send from the file; that works once the site is live.
4. **Phone check.** Open the file on your phone and send Claude 3 screenshots (top, middle, contact or form). Claude can't see a real phone, so it checks your screenshots and fixes what it sees.
5. **Rounds of changes.** Paste the client's notes into the chat (or say "check my email from [client]"). Claude lists the changes, makes them, and shows the next version with what changed. Your price includes 2 rounds. Past that, Claude stops and drafts the extra-round email for the client (email 5); it makes the changes once you tell it the client said yes.
6. **Track it:** Stage "Building". Claude ends each round with a line for the Log tab; copy it from the box and paste it into the first empty row.

**Tips**
- Short, specific feedback works best: "make the prices bigger", "add Saturday hours".
- If Claude asks a question, answer it in the next message.
- If something looks wrong on a phone, attach a screenshot and say what's wrong.
