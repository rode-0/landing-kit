# 5. Build and review

1. **Start a new chat** in your Websites Project (Opus 5.5 or newer). Paste the client's answers and attach their logo and photos. If Gmail is connected, add: "Check my emails with [client email] too."
2. **Version 1.** Claude writes a short brief about the client's customers, builds the page, checks it, and shows it to you. Want options first? Say "show me concepts" for three quick layout ideas.
3. **Show the client.** Click **Publish** on the page and send the link (email 3 in `templates/emails.md`).
4. **Rounds of changes.** Paste the client's notes into the chat (or say "check my email from [client]"). Claude lists the changes, makes them, and shows the next version with what changed. Your price includes 2 rounds; from round 3, Claude reminds you that the round may be billable (email 5).
5. **Track it:** Stage "Building". Claude ends each round with a line for the Log tab; paste it in.

**Tips**
- Short, specific feedback works best: "make the prices bigger", "add Saturday hours".
- If Claude asks a question, answer it in the next message.
- If something looks wrong on a phone, attach a screenshot and say what's wrong.
