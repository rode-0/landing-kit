# 9. Back up

You don't need special tools. Three things cover it:

- **Netlify keeps every version.** In a site's **Deploys** page you can put an earlier version back live.
- **Claude keeps your drafts.** Each client's chat has every version.
- **One folder per client** in Google Drive or Dropbox: their answers, logo, photos, and every final page named by date (`2026-11-10 index.html`). If anything else is lost, this folder is enough to rebuild. It's also what you hand over if a client moves on.

Keep the folder link in the tracker's Clients tab.
