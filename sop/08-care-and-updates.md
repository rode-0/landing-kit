# 8. Care and updates

1. The client emails or texts what changed (new hours, prices, a holiday, photos). Small updates up to the number in your agreement are included each month; anything bigger, or more than that, is quoted first.
2. Open their chat in Claude (or a new chat in the Project with the latest page attached) and say what changed. Holiday closures go in as a notice with an end date; the site hides it automatically afterward.
3. Download the new page and drag its folder onto the site's **Deploys** page in Netlify.
4. Save the new `index.html` in the client's backup folder, named by date. Log the update.

**Every few months:** check in with each care client, and tap the outside links on their site (booking, store, socials) to make sure they still work. It's a good moment to ask for a referral or a review.

**Domain renewals:** the Dashboard flags domains renewing in the next 60 days. Send email 8 a few weeks ahead.
