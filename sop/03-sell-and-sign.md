# 3. Sell and sign

**Set your prices once** with the pricing page (`tools/pricing`). It works out what each site costs you and what you earn per hour, and writes a quote you can send.

**The package:**
- **New website**, one-time price, including **2 rounds of changes**.
- **Extra rounds of changes**, a set price each (the calculator suggests one from your hourly rate).
- **Monthly care**: hosting, the contact form, and a set number of small updates a month (you choose it on the pricing page; write it in the agreement).
- **The client buys their own domain**, in their name.

**The first meeting (15 minutes):**
1. "When someone finds you online, what do you want them to do?" (Call, book, walk in, order.)
2. "What do customers usually ask before they buy?" This shapes the whole site.
3. **"What do you already have?"** Their current website and who can log in to it; their domain, where it's registered and who has the login; email on that domain; booking, ordering, store or payment tools; Google Business Profile; social pages. **Reuse what works:** link to their booking or store page instead of replacing it. If they have email on their domain, it must keep working (see step 6). Ask for access invitations, never passwords.
4. Explain the package, price and timing (a first draft within about 5 business days of getting their details).
5. Ask for their logo and 4 to 10 photos.

**Then send** (Claude can draft this email: [`templates/emails.md`](../templates/emails.md), email 1):
- the question page link,
- the agreement ([`templates/agreement.md`](../templates/agreement.md)),
- an invoice for a **50% deposit**, made with `tools/invoice` or your Square/Stripe/PayPal account.

**Track it:** Stage "Meeting", then "Deposit paid" once the deposit arrives. Log the payment.
