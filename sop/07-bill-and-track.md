# 7. Bill and track

**Invoices**
- **One-off invoices** (deposit, final payment, extra rounds): make them with `tools/invoice` (Save as PDF) or in Square, Stripe or PayPal.
- **Monthly care:** set up an automatic monthly invoice in Square, Stripe or PayPal, so the client's card is charged each month.
- **Extra rounds:** bill them with the final payment, or as they happen.

**Your dashboard** (`tracker.xlsx`, in Google Sheets or Excel)
- **Dashboard tab:** clients by stage, money this month and year, unpaid invoices, hours, money per hour, extra rounds to bill, domains renewing soon, and a chart of income by month.
- **Clients tab:** one row per client. Move them through the Stage column.
- **Log tab:** every payment, invoice, round and update. Claude gives you the lines to paste.

**Once a week:** open the Dashboard. Chase anything orange (unpaid, renewing soon, extra rounds to bill).
