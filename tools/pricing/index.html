Good to know

  • How many sites your Claude plan covers isn't a fixed number. Claude's limits reset every few hours and weekly. After your first two or three sites, check how much of your weekly limit each one used, then plan around that.
  • Use Claude Opus 5.5 or newer. It uses your limit faster than smaller models, and it makes better sites.
  • The client pays for their domain (usually $10 to $20 a year) and owns it.
  • Contact forms are free for up to 250 messages a month per business (Web3Forms).
  • Hosting: host every client in your own Netlify account (Personal or Pro) and include it in the monthly care price. Netlify's free plan allows only one person per account and runs out after roughly 20 updates a month.
  • Getting paid: set up the monthly care price as an automatic monthly invoice (Square, Stripe or PayPal all do this), so the client's card is charged each month.
